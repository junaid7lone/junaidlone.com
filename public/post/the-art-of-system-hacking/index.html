TL;DR: Hacking systems isn’t about tools—it’s about understanding how things fit together.

The Three-Layer Model

Every system (technical or social) breaks down into three layers:

  1. The Visible Layer - What you see on the surface. APIs, UIs, documented procedures.
  2. The Structural Layer - How components connect. Network topology, permissions, trust relationships.
  3. The Assumption Layer - What the designers believed would never happen.

The hack always lives in the gap between the Structural and Assumption layers.

Practical Exercise

Next time you’re trying to “get into” something (a system, an organization, a codebase), map these three layers on paper before firing up Burp or writing a script.

You’ll find the attack surface shrinks dramatically—and the paths become obvious.
