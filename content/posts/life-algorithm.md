I want a system that does one thing well:

**make the right opportunities easier to find.**

Not by shouting louder.
Not by posting random content into the void.
Not by pretending every DM is a strategy.

By building a signal that is:

- clear on the website
- consistent on social
- searchable in email
- easy to forward in a personal message
- automated enough that it doesn’t rot when I’m busy

## The stack

The basic loop is simple:

1. **Website** — a clean home base that says what I do and what I’m open to.
2. **Blog** — deeper thoughts, proof of work, experiments, and useful notes.
3. **Social posts** — short versions of the same signal.
4. **Email** — owned follow-up and relationship building.
5. **Personal messages** — targeted, specific, human.
6. **Automation** — repurpose, publish, capture, and remind.

## What this is really for

I’m not trying to be visible to everyone.
I’m trying to be *obvious* to the right people.

That means the site should make it easy for someone to answer:

- What does he do?
- Is this relevant to me?
- How do I reach him?
- Why now?

If those answers are unclear, the system is weak.

## The rule

One source of truth.
Many formats.
Same direction.

If a thought is worth keeping, it should be able to become:

- a blog post
- a LinkedIn post
- a short email update
- a direct email note
- a direct outreach note

The machine should do the repackaging.
I should do the thinking.

## What I’m building toward

A quiet engine that keeps working even when I’m not.

One that brings in:

- interesting collaborators
- clients
- investors
- builders
- people who know something I don’t
- opportunities worth saying yes to

That’s the game.
Not noise. Not vanity.
Signal.
